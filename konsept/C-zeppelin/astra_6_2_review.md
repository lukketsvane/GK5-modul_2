**Use nine transverse-axis louvers, synchronized by one enclosed rack.** Each grey scoop is a left and right wing clamped to the same short steel shaft. The split lets a continuous black spine pass through the waist without requiring a moving slot in the spine. The wings overlap *in side elevation*, not by rubbing or nesting. Turning a knob at the narrow end brings all nine scoops more upright, opening sightlines between them while the light source stays still.

### Geometry for the solids model

Use millimetres; \(x\) runs nose to tail, \(y\) along the shafts, and \(z\) upward from the table. Put the nine pivot axes at \(x=i(13\pi)\), \(i=-4,\ldots,4\), \(z=116\). The unusual 40.84 pitch is deliberate: it is exactly 13 rack teeth. Model the fixed spine from \(x=-188\) to \(188\), within \(|y|\le12\). Support its underside on a hollow central pedestal, also within \(|y|\le12\), on a Ø110 × 8 base.

For a wing at angle \(\theta\) from vertical, define local chord and face-normal directions
\[
u=(\sin\theta,0,\cos\theta),\qquad n=(\cos\theta,0,-\sin\theta).
\]
From the centre station outward, use chord half-lengths \(a=[86,82,72,58,38]\) and wingtip distances from the centreline \(W=[68,64,57,46,32]\). Mirror the stations about \(x=0\). For each half-wing, let \(s=|y|\) run from 16 to \(W\). From 16 to 26 the sheet is flat, spanning chord coordinates \(-a\) to \(+a\), with its midsurface 5 ahead of the axis along \(n\). Beyond 26 set \(t=(s-26)/(W-26)\), reduce the half-chord to \(a\sqrt{1-t^2}\), and add normal offset \(160-\sqrt{160^2-(s-26)^2}\). This gives a rounded, shallow cylindrical scoop rather than a compound surface that cannot be rolled from sheet.

In CadQuery or build123d, intersect a 0.5-thick cylindrical shell with the extruded half-ellipse trimming profile and unite it to the tangent flat root. For the cutting blank, use *arc length*, not projected width, across the bend. Use grey-finished aluminium, deburred with rounded corners. Bolt each flat root to a printed split-clamp shoe on a Ø5 shaft; put two sheet screws near chord coordinates ±10, clear of the shaft bore. Keep the shoe, screw ends and sheet collectively between normal coordinates −7 and +12, and the shoe outside \(|y|=14\).

### Drive and motion

Fit two 5 × 10 × 4 bearings per station in aligned spine cheeks and a module-1, 20-tooth, 20° pressure-angle spur pinion between them. Use a matching straight rack, teeth downward, pitch line 10 above shaft height. Provide a backed sliding guide with adjustable mesh depth; the rack must not lift out under torque. A 360-long rack fits under the spine cover including travel. The pinions can all have identical tooth phase. Fixture the shoes at the closed angle before tightening their shaft clamps. Locate shafts axially with clips and inner-race spacers, not by squeezing the bearing outer races.

Extend the \(i=4\) shaft through the narrow end station to a Ø36 knob occupying \(y=72\) to 79. The extended shaft passes behind the offset sheet. With the rack guided in translation, there are nine revolute coordinates and one slider coordinate; nine independent gear meshes leave **one ideal DOF**. At the upper mesh, rack displacement is \(10\,\Delta\theta\) with angle in radians. Rack stops set **38° closed to 10° open**, just 4.89 travel. At the middle station the projected sheet chord at the flat root shrinks from 106 to 30 against the 40.84 spacing: this is a visible opening, not just a change in highlight. A friction washer at the knob can hold intermediate settings; the gearing is not self-locking.

### Clearance, light and verification

At any common angle, adjacent pivot centres are separated along \(n\) by at least \(40.84\cos38^\circ=32.18\). Subtracting the entire 19-wide moving slab leaves **13.18 nominal separation** between stations. Allow 1° independent angular error per shaft (less than 1.6 normal excursion per wing) and 1 additional displacement per station for fabrication and flex: the residual exceeds 7.9. These are **acceptance allowances**, not measured PETG or sheet performance. The spine-to-shoe gap is 2 nominal; limit combined lateral runout, end float and frame error to 1 for a minimum 1 running gap, excluding intended shaft and spacer interfaces. The lowest wing stays above \(z=29\) nominal, clear of the base. The end stations govern length: even using the full positive normal allowance, \(163.37+38\sin38^\circ+12<200\). Including knob and base, the nominal assembly fits 400 × 160 × 300.

Mount warm-white low-voltage LED strips on fixed aluminium lands along the lower spine, aimed down and outward under the wings. Clip the 0.5 diffuser film to the fixed lands, within the spine width. Separate the LED cable channel physically from the pinions; take wiring through a grommet and strain relief down the pedestal to an external listed, current-limited supply. No moving conductor is needed. Cover the rack and gears; the exposed louver gaps still warrant a pinch assessment.

**Test plan:** Sweep the actual solids, including screw heads, shaft retainers, knob and diffuser clips, at 0.5° increments. Check minimum distances as well as intersections, excluding only named mating interfaces. For wing pairs, use the nearer sample: at radius below 88, the unsampled 0.25° adds under 0.39 motion per body. Deduct that margin from sampled clearances. Check tooth engagement separately using actual involute profiles, backlash and guide tolerances, not pitch-cylinder interference. Build three adjacent full-size centre stations first; measure play on reversal, lateral runout and minimum gaps. Then assemble all nine, measure knob torque and phase error at both stops, cycle 500 times and inspect for clamp slip, rubbing and rack wear. Run the light at full power to thermal equilibrium and compare measured LED, film and fitting temperatures with their material ratings. CAD does not prove acceptable hand force, formed-sheet stiffness, thermal life, stability against a knob pull or safe finger access.

An axial accordion would change length more dramatically, but a single slider does not itself distribute seven to nine fin positions. It needs additional spacing links or staged stops and room for the collapsed stack. Fixed pivot centres and one rack make this silhouette and its clearances easier to build and verify.
